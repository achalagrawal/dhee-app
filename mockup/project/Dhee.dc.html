About Dhee

“Dhee” (धी) is an old word for the reflective intellect, the faculty of understanding and discernment. Not cleverness, but the quiet capacity to see a thing clearly.

Most tools rush to answer. Dhee is built to slow down with you: to ask what you actually mean, to offer an honest point of view, and to hand the question back so you can keep living with it. It won't pretend to be your therapist, your guru, or the final word. It's a thinking-partner, one that takes your questions seriously.

We built it warm and plainspoken on purpose. The big questions are hard enough without jargon.
