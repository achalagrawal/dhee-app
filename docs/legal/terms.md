Dhee is run by **Achal Agrawal**, an individual in India — not a company. "We"

Anyone can use Dhee, from anywhere. It is built and run from India, and these

are, that is your call to make and not ours to police.

## Who can use it

You must be **18 or older**. Dhee is not designed for children, and we have no
way to obtain the parental consent Indian law requires for anyone under 18.

You need an account to use Dhee. You sign in with a code sent to your email
address, or with Google. Keep access to that email account secure — anyone who
can read your inbox can sign in as you. One account is meant for one person;
please do not share it.

## Not professional advice

Dhee is not a therapist, doctor, lawyer, financial adviser, or crisis service,
and nothing it says is professional advice. For decisions with real
consequences, consult a qualified professional. What you choose to do is yours
to decide and yours to own.

Nothing you write to Dhee is privileged or confidential in the legal sense.
There is no doctor-patient, therapist-client, or lawyer-client relationship
here.

## In an emergency

If you are in danger, or thinking about harming yourself or someone else,
contact your local emergency number or a crisis line immediately. **Dhee cannot
provide emergency help.** It cannot call anyone for you, it is not monitored by
a human, and no one is watching your conversation for signs that you need help.
See [Safety & limitations](./safety.md) for resources.

## What Dhee can and can't do

Dhee generates its replies with a language model. It can be wrong, incomplete,
one-sided, or confidently mistaken. It draws on a body of philosophical texts,
and its reading of them is its own — we do not guarantee that any reply
faithfully represents that tradition or any other.

Treat what it says as a starting point for your own thinking, not as fact and
not as instruction.

Dhee also forms and keeps an evolving picture of who you are, which shapes its
later replies. You can read, correct, and delete that picture at any time from
the "Dhee's understanding of you" screen; the Privacy Policy explains how it
works.

## Acceptable use

- to break the law, or to help anyone else do so;
- to generate content that abuses, harasses, defames, sexualizes, or endangers

- to extract, scrape, or resell the underlying texts, model output, or the

- to probe, overload, or work around our limits, security, or account system;
- to present Dhee's output as professional advice to someone else.

We may limit, suspend, or end access that does any of this.

## Your content

What you write stays yours. You keep ownership of your messages and everything
you add to your profile.

To run the service, you give us permission to store your content, send it to
the providers listed in the Privacy Policy so a reply can be generated, and
derive from it the notes Dhee keeps about you. That permission is not exclusive,
and it ends for any content when you delete it.

You also give us permission to read your content in order to judge how well Dhee
is answering and to improve it, as set out in
[Reading conversations to improve Dhee](./privacy.md#reading-conversations-to-improve-dhee).
That is the one use beyond operating Dhee for you, and the Privacy Policy is
where its limits are written down.

You are responsible for what you send — including for not sending other people's
personal information, or anything you are not free to share. Please do not put
private details about yourself or anyone else into a conversation: it is read.

## Plans, limits, and upgrades

Dhee is free to use. **There is no paid plan and no payment today** — nothing
here asks you for money, and we will not charge you without saying so first and
asking you to agree to it.

Answering you costs us money on every message, so we may introduce usage limits
— a cap on messages in a day, most likely — and we may change them as we learn
what a reasonable day looks like. If we do, the app will show you where you
stand rather than failing silently, and you will be able to ask us for more.

## Availability

Dhee is offered as it is, without a service-level promise. We may change,
suspend, or discontinue any part of it, and the underlying model may be
replaced by another. We will try not to surprise you with changes that lose your
data.

## Ending your account

You can stop using Dhee at any time. You can delete your conversations and
everything Dhee remembers from inside the app, and you can ask us to close your
account entirely by writing to **privacy@dhee.app**.
Closing an account deletes what it held: the profile and photo, the
conversations, and everything Dhee had inferred about you.

We may suspend or terminate an account that breaches these terms, or where we
are required to. Where it is reasonable to do so, we will tell you why and give
you a chance to get your content out first.
